# Survey: documented trials on the Clextral Evolum family (2026-09-26)

This is a research survey, not a blind-test case. Found via WebSearch (Firecrawl is out
of credits; WebFetch is blocked network-wide in this session), so entries are as
summarized by the search tool, with the source link for each. Read the source directly
before relying on any number here for a decision.

Our own machine's model ("25") is still unconfirmed from a nameplate; entries below
cover 25, 32 and larger Evolum variants so the table is useful regardless of which one
ours turns out to be.

## Evolum 32 / HT32 and larger

- No trial with published numeric parameters was found in this pass; only equipment
  listings (Exapro used-machine listing for an EV032 M; Clextral's own commercial pages).
  The EV032 M is described as a 40D extruder with a barrel of 10 segments of 128 mm each.
- Evolum 44+ is named for snacks/cereals at industrial throughput (Clextral's own
  marketing copy states "5000+ lbs/hr" and up to 23,000 kg/h for the largest lines); no
  independent trial data found.

## Same product family, machine not confirmed as Evolum (flagged, do not treat as same line)

- **WPI + casein in high-protein nutrition bars**: a twin-screw extruder, **29 mm**
  screw, L/D 40, 8 heating zones (25/35/45/50-150 variable/45/25 °C), feed moisture 40%,
  "feed rate 60 r/min" (likely a mistranslation of screw speed, not an actual feed rate
  in kg/h — the number and unit don't match a mass flow). 29 mm is not a standard Evolum
  screw diameter (Clextral's line runs 25, 32, 44...), so this is very likely a different
  make (common in Chinese literature: SLG, Brabender or similar). **Flagged as a
  different machine family — closest on paper to our chrupka (casein + WPI together)
  but not verified as an Evolum.** Source: ScienceDirect S2590157523001621.
- Journal of Dairy Science, "Functionality of Extrusion-Texturized Whey Proteins":
  machine not identified in the search summary.

## What is still missing

- No trial found yet with **pure WPI alone** (no starch, no casein blend) on a confirmed
  Evolum machine.
- No trial found yet that varies **feed rate** as an independent factor on any Evolum
  (all found trials hold feed rate fixed and vary moisture, speed or temperature instead).
- The two most promising dairy-specific leads (MDPI book chapter, Blanco Espeso thesis)
  are known to exist but their methods have not been read yet — blocked by the current
  Firecrawl/WebFetch outage, not by absence of the source.

## Caveat on this survey

Every row above except cases 02 and 05 (already fully read and, where used, sealed) is
transcribed from WebSearch's own synthesized summary, not from the source document
itself. WebSearch is not filtered for blind extraction — its summaries can and did
already include result-level statements (e.g., the WPI/casein bar study's conclusion
about sulfhydryl and hardening was in the same summary as its methods). **None of this
survey is used, or should be used, as a sealed blind-test case.** It is a map of where
to look next, not evidence.
